## Firestick setup (both providers)

1. Install **Downloader** from the Amazon App Store
2. Enable "Apps from Unknown Sources" in Firestick settings
3. In Downloader, enter the code your provider sends (both send a player code in the welcome email)
4. Install **TiviMate** or **IPTV Smarters Pro**
5. Open the player, choose "Xtream Codes API," enter the username, password, and server URL from your provider email
6. Wait 2–4 minutes for EPG to populate
7. Pin your top 10 channels — the full 25,000 list is overwhelming on first load

## Why IPTV buffers (and how to fix it)

Buffering on IPTV is rarely the provider's fault alone. The three causes, in order of frequency:

1. **ISP throttling during peak** — most UK ISPs throttle streaming traffic between 7–11pm. Fix: enable the VPN included with your subscription, or test on mobile hotspot to confirm.
2. **Wi-Fi congestion** — Firestick on 2.4GHz in a flat with 10 other networks will buffer regardless of provider. Fix: 5GHz band, or Ethernet via the Firestick adapter.
3. **Provider oversubscription** — the server can't handle peak load. Fix: this is the one you can't fix. Switch providers. This is why the test above logs buffer events per match — it separates cause 1 and 2 from cause 3.
